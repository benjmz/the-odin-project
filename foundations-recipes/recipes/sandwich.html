Sandwich

A sandwich is a great lunch option! Sandwiches are typically served with lettuce, tomato, and meat in the middle. Condiments are typically used.

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Lettuce
  • tomato
  • Cheese
  • Meat (Turkey, Chicken, Ham, Steak)

Steps

  1. Add any condiments to your bread
  2. Add lettuce, cheese, meat, and tomatoes in that order
  3. Enjoy!
