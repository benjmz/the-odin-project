Coffee

Coffee is one of the best drinks ever. Made from grinding coffee beans and saturating water, many cultures drink this drink in the morning, after meals, or even before going to bed.

Ingredients

  • Coffee beans
  • Water
  • Coffee maker

Steps

  1. Grind beans in grinder.
  2. Place beans in coffee filter
  3. Fill water accordingly
  4. Turn on coffee and enjoy :)
