Ice Cream

Ice cream is a delicious dessert made up of milk and sugar. There are various flavors to choose from. Some classics are: vanilla, strawberry, and chocolate.

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Sugar

Steps

  1. Plug in your Ninja Creami machine.
  2. Put ingredients in.
  3. Turn on ninja creami and press creamify :p
  4. You made ice cream.
